Fonts shipped with Vice Cover Studio
====================================

All four files are released under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(see OFL.txt in this directory). None of them is a Rockstar Games font.

- NotoSans-Regular.ttf
  Noto Sans, Regular weight. Used for the editor interface text and for
  caption subtitles on the cover.

- NotoSans-Bold.ttf
  Noto Sans, Bold weight. Used for the editor interface and for caption
  subtitles on the cover.

- NotoSansDisplay-CondensedBlack.ttf
  Noto Sans Display, Condensed Black weight. Used for the big condensed
  cover title in all three lettering styles ("Vice Display" 900).

- NotoSansDisplay-CondensedBold.ttf
  Noto Sans Display, Condensed Bold weight. Used for the cover title at
  weight 700 where the design calls for it ("Vice Display" 700).
